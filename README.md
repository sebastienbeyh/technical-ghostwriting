## Working Method

Each engagement begins with agreement on the deliverable, intended audience, scope, source material and acceptance criteria.

My working method emphasizes:

1. **Purpose and audience:** Establish what the document must communicate and to whom.
2. **Source review:** Examine available material and identify information gaps.
3. **Document architecture:** Develop a structure appropriate to the subject and deliverable.
4. **Technical discipline:** Distinguish established facts, assumptions, proposed approaches and unresolved questions.
5. **Consistency:** Maintain coherent terminology, notation and document logic.
6. **Evidence:** Check technical claims against available documentation and authoritative sources where appropriate.
7. **Editorial review:** Review the completed document for clarity, accuracy, structure and consistency.

Polished language cannot substitute for engineering validity. Where evidence is unavailable, limitations should be stated rather than concealed.

## Engagement Principles

* **Clear scope:** Deliverables, exclusions and responsibilities are agreed before work begins.
* **Technical integrity:** Claims are not fabricated to make a document appear more persuasive.
* **Source discipline:** Technical assertions and references should be verifiable.
* **Confidentiality:** Client information is handled according to agreed engagement terms.
* **Authorship and attribution:** Ghostwriting, intellectual property ownership and publication arrangements are agreed with the client.
* **Defined revisions:** Review cycles and acceptance criteria are established according to the assignment.

Contractual terms should address confidentiality, intellectual property, attribution, permitted use and any applicable publication restrictions.
